# Content pillar: **How to Find Backlink Opportunities**

This is the best pillar because it connects naturally to:

**free tools** → “use this tool to find X”  
**alternative articles** → “manual way vs tool way”  
**product intent** → people searching this are already trying to get links/mentions

Avoid broad “link building guide” content at first. Too competitive, too generic, and harder to convert. Go after specific “how to find…” queries.

## 10 sub-pillar articles

### 1\. How to Find Websites That Mention Your Competitors But Not You

**Intent:** high commercial intent  
**Mentiohunt angle:** competitor mention tracking / prospecting  
**Free tool tie-in:** competitor mention finder

This is probably one of the strongest articles because the pain is obvious: “they linked or mentioned a competitor, so maybe they’ll mention me too.”

---

### 2\. How to Find Listicles Where Your Product Should Be Included

**Intent:** very strong for SaaS/founders  
**Mentiohunt angle:** find “best X tools” articles  
**Free tool tie-in:** listicle opportunity finder

Example searches:

> “best customer feedback tools”  
> “top AI writing tools”  
> “best backlink tools”

These pages already rank and already mention products. Perfect opportunity.

---

### 3\. How to Find “Alternative To” Pages for Your SaaS

**Intent:** strong SEO/link intent  
**Mentiohunt angle:** find pages comparing competitors  
**Free tool tie-in:** alternative page finder

This connects perfectly with your own alternative articles strategy.

Example:

> If your competitor is GummySearch, look for “GummySearch alternatives”, “sites like GummySearch”, “GummySearch competitors.”

---

### 4\. How to Find Resource Pages That Could Link to Your Website

**Intent:** classic link building  
**Mentiohunt angle:** resource page discovery  
**Free tool tie-in:** resource page finder

Good for directories, startup resources, marketing tool lists, niche curated pages, etc.

---

### 5\. How to Find Directory Submission Opportunities for Your Startup

**Intent:** very beginner-friendly, high traffic potential  
**Mentiohunt angle:** directory opportunity discovery  
**Free tool tie-in:** startup directory finder

This can rank well because founders constantly search for ways to get early backlinks.

You can also create supporting free tools like:

> SaaS Directory Finder  
> Startup Directory Finder  
> AI Tool Directory Finder

---

### 6\. How to Find Blogs That Write About Products Like Yours

**Intent:** mid/high intent  
**Mentiohunt angle:** topical prospecting  
**Free tool tie-in:** blog prospect finder

This is broader than competitor mentions but still useful.

Example:

> “Find blogs that write about project management tools”  
> “Find blogs that review AI SEO tools”

---

### 7\. How to Find Guest Post Opportunities in Your Niche

**Intent:** traffic-heavy, but competitive  
**Mentiohunt angle:** prospect discovery, not generic guest posting advice  
**Free tool tie-in:** guest post opportunity finder

Be careful with this one. It’s competitive and attracts some low-quality SEO people, but it has demand.

Make the article specific:

> “How to Find Guest Post Opportunities Without Using Spammy Footprints”

---

### 8\. How to Find Broken Backlink Opportunities for Your Website

**Intent:** strong SEO audience  
**Mentiohunt angle:** could be future feature, or educational content  
**Free tool tie-in:** broken backlink checker/finder if you build one

This is a bit more Ahrefs/Semrush territory, so I’d only prioritize it if you have or plan a tool around it.

---

### 9\. How to Find Unlinked Brand Mentions

**Intent:** very high commercial intent  
**Mentiohunt angle:** brand mention monitoring  
**Free tool tie-in:** unlinked mention finder

This one is excellent, but only if Mentiohunt supports brand mention discovery.

People searching this already understand the value:

> “They mentioned me, but didn’t link to me. I should ask for the link.”

---

### 10\. How to Build a Backlink Prospect List From Your Competitors

**Intent:** strong, tactical  
**Mentiohunt angle:** competitor-based prospecting  
**Free tool tie-in:** backlink prospect list generator

This should be a cornerstone article because it combines multiple opportunity types:

- competitor mentions
- competitor backlinks
- alternative pages
- listicles
- directories
- review pages
- partner pages

This article can naturally push Mentiohunt as the easier way to do it.
