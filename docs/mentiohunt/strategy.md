# Mentiohunt — Core Strategy

## Core strategic decision

**Mentiohunt should focus on a backlinks-only autopilot product** rather than community mention monitoring.

### Why this focus is stronger

- Backlink acquisition is a clearer, already-budgeted SEO spend.
- The market is showing demand for **managed placements**, not just DIY outreach tooling.
- Social/community reply automation has rising platform risk and moderation friction.
- Monitoring without handling the reply end-to-end creates weaker perceived value.
- A narrower promise is easier to explain, sell, fulfill, and optimize.

### Category definition

Mentiohunt is not just another outreach tool. It should be positioned as:

**A managed backlink placement autopilot**

or

**done-for-you backlink acquisition with approval control**

---

## Recommendation

The cleanest version of Mentiohunt:

- **Primary ICP:** founder-led B2B SaaS
- **Core offer:** managed backlink placements
- **Primary promise:** approval-based control without outreach workload
- **Differentiation:** transparent, relevance-first, done-for-you execution
- **Strategic wedge:** more transparent than an agency, less work than outreach software
- **Secondary expansion path:** agencies/resellers

This is tighter, easier to explain, and more commercially legible than keeping community mentions as a parallel feature.
