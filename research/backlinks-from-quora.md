Getting backlinks from Quora currently revolves more around driving referral traffic and building "Human Consensus" signals for Google rather than directly boosting Domain Authority through "dofollow" links.

Because Quora links are virtually all `nofollow`, their primary SEO value in 2026 comes from establishing brand authority in niche conversations that Google now prioritizes in Search Generative Experience (SGE) and LLM training data.

### 1\. Shift from Link Drops to "Value First" Strategy

The consensus across SEO communities is that old-school "link dropping" (posting a short answer just to link out) will get you banned or your content collapsed.

- The "Hub-and-Spoke" method: Answer top-performing questions in your niche with 300+ words of genuinely helpful content. Use a link only as a "deep dive" resource at the end, rather than the primary point of the post.
- Targeting "Parasite SEO" opportunities: Look for Quora threads that already rank on the first page of Google for your target keywords. Providing a high-value answer on an existing, high-ranking thread is more effective than starting new ones.
- Avoid the "Spam Claim" hammer: Quora has become extremely aggressive with AI-spam filters. Users report [losing accounts with over 1 million views](https://bsky.app/profile/findfocus.bsky.social/post/3m4pupgdsyz2i) simply for linking out to their own blogs too frequently.

### 2\. Quora Spaces for Indexing and SEO

Quora Spaces (similar to Facebook Groups or Subreddits) are increasingly used for "Parasite SEO" because they often rank and index faster than individual answers.

- Community signals: Some experts suggest that [community discussion is the only un-gameable metric left](https://x.com/RituWithAI/status/2004515729614520365), and active engagement in Quora Spaces helps signal to Google that your brand is a trusted authority in its niche.
- Interlinking: Share your Quora answers within relevant "Spaces" to increase internal traffic and strengthen the internal link profile of your post, which helps keep the answer from "dying" in the SERPs.

### 3\. Best Practices to Avoid Bans

To maintain an account while building links, you must simulate "natural" community behavior.

- The 5-to-1 Rule: Post 5 high-quality, helpful answers that contain _zero_ links for every 1 answer that includes a link to your site.
- Profile Optimization: Fill out your Quora bio completely. In 2026, [profile backlinks](https://bsky.app/profile/alexhustles.bsky.social/post/3lbrlydaka42b) are still considered "easy wins" for a natural backlink profile, even if they are nofollow.
- Use Branded Anchors: When you do link, use your brand name or the specific title of the article. Avoid exact-match commercial keywords (e.g., "best cheap sunglasses") as these are high-signals for spam reviewers.

### 4\. Why Nofollow Links Still Matter in 2026

While they don't pass traditional "link juice," Quora links are valuable for:

- Direct Referral Traffic: High-intent users searching for specific solutions on Quora often convert at higher rates than cold organic traffic.
- LLM Visibility: It is widely believed that [LLMs like ChatGPT and Claude scrape Quora](https://x.com/mattaskumar/status/1951523890884145537) to understand human intent. Being present there ensures your brand is mentioned when users ask AI for recommendations.
- Natural Link Profile: A healthy backlink profile should not be 100% dofollow. Having [high-traffic nofollow mentions](https://www.reddit.com/r/SEO_LLM/comments/1qy3pb8/do_nofollow_links_actually_pass_any_ranking/) acts as a "hint" to Google that your site is a legitimate part of the web's conversation.

## Tactics people use

A common tactic is to search Quora questions via Google using `site:quora.com + keyword`, then answer the most relevant threads. Another approach is to use SEO tools to find Quora pages already ranking for long-tail keywords, then answer those threads because they tend to get indexed faster. Some marketers also place the Quora URL on an internal “find us online” page to help Google discover and index the answer link faster. ## Writing answers

The answer should read like a helpful mini-article, not an ad. A strong structure is: short direct answer, 2–4 useful tips, then one contextual link if it adds real depth. You can also mention alternatives, competitors, or common mistakes to make the answer feel balanced and less promotional. [linkedin](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/ehab-shaikh_how-to-get-a-valuebale-link-from-quora-activity-7346448468904701954-nTyE)

## What to avoid

Don’t paste the same answer everywhere, don’t link first, and don’t use Quora as a mass automation channel. Avoid thin answers, irrelevant links, and any behavior that looks like spam, because the whole strategy depends on trust and usefulness. Also remember that Quora links are typically nofollow, so the main upside is often referral traffic, indexing, and brand visibility rather than direct PageRank transfer. ## Best use cases

Quora is strongest when you want to support a broader link-building mix, build topical authority, or drive targeted traffic to educational content, tools, or comparison pages. It is weaker as a standalone backlink strategy and works best when combined with other earned links and content distribution. [diib](https://diib.com/learn/does-quora-help-seo/)

## A practical workflow

- Pick 20–50 niche questions already relevant to your product or content.
- Write one genuinely helpful answer per question.
- Add one useful link only where it improves the answer.
- Refresh and engage on answers that start getting views.
- Reuse the topics to find similar questions and build topical coverage. [codyschneider](https://codyschneider.com/quora-backlinks/)
