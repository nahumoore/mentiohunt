Elevation Vibe is a blog covering goal-setting and growth mindset, competing for attention against established names like James Clear and Lifehack. In its first month running Mentiohunt, it earned 3 live backlinks — each from an independent blog, each pointing to a specific Elevation Vibe article that matched the topic.

![Backlink checker showing Elevation Vibe's referring domains growing from 0 to 3 in its first month, listing sunsama.com, weefer.co.id, and techcapitalnews.in as the linking sites](/resources/case-studies/elevationvibe/backlink-profile-proof.webp)

## Where Elevation Vibe started

Elevation Vibe's target keywords are all clustered around one theme: goal setting and growth mindset — "goal setting tips," "growth mindset blog," "personal growth resources," "how to set goals," "mindset development." Its competitor set for discovery included sites like [jamesclear.com](https://jamesclear.com), [lifehack.org](https://lifehack.org), and [developgoodhabits.com](https://developgoodhabits.com) — a crowded, well-established niche for a young blog with no existing backlink profile.

## The approach

Mentiohunt's daily discovery methods worked against that keyword set, surfacing pages already writing about goal-setting and productivity that didn't yet reference Elevation Vibe. Each qualified opportunity got a ready-to-send outreach email tied to the specific Elevation Vibe article that fit the page's content.

## What this doesn't prove

This is one company, one month, and 3 backlinks. It's not a guarantee of a specific number of links, replies, or rankings for any other product — results depend heavily on the niche and how much existing content overlap there is with the sites being targeted. What it shows is that a brand-new blog with no prior backlink profile can start earning contextual, on-topic links in its first month without a founder manually prospecting sites one at a time.
