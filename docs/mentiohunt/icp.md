# Mentiohunt — ICP

## Primary ICP — Tier 1A: Bootstrap / Indie SaaS Founder

**Bootstrapped or pre-Series A SaaS founder who owns SEO personally**

### Buyer

- Founder
- Co-founder

### Company profile

- B2B SaaS, bootstrapped or seed-stage
- 1–10 person team
- Has content or landing pages worth ranking
- Already believes SEO matters
- Cannot justify agency minimums ($1K–5K/mo) and has no time for DIY

### The pricing dead zone this segment falls into

Agency minimums price them out. DIY tactics require 5–15 hrs/week they don't have. Freelancers ghost or deliver junk. No managed, autopilot option exists under $500/mo — that is the gap Mentiohunt fills for this segment.

### Buying triggers

- Organic growth has plateaued
- They tried DIY outreach and burned out
- Hired a freelancer who disappeared or delivered garbage
- Agency quoted $2K–5K/mo minimum — out of budget
- Publishing content but earning no backlinks

### Why this tier is strong

- Very high intent signal — active buying frustration, not passive awareness
- Fast decision-making, no procurement friction
- Founder-to-founder resonance in messaging
- High sensitivity to time savings
- Volume potential as SaaS ecosystem grows

### Budget signal

$200–500/mo. Needs productized tiers. Will pay per link or per month if output is verifiable.

## Primary ICP — Tier 1B: Solo SEO Manager at Sub-$3M ARR B2B SaaS

**Single person owning all of SEO at a lean SaaS company — has the knowledge, lacks the bandwidth**

### Buyer

- SEO Lead
- Head of Growth
- Growth Manager
- Content + SEO

### Company profile

- B2B SaaS, seed to Series A/B
- 5–30 person team
- Has a content program and understands link building strategy
- Cannot justify building outreach ops internally
- Has tried agencies and been disappointed with opacity or quality

### Core pain

They are the single point of failure for a function that requires 20–30 hrs/month of manual work. Tools like Pitchbox and Respona exist but require dedicated operation — they are not self-running. This persona wants to offload execution while retaining visibility and the ability to cancel anything that doesn't fit their brand.

### Buying triggers

- Struggling to scale link building without burning out
- Manages prospecting, outreach, negotiation, and verification alone
- Outreach tools require more campaign ownership than they can give
- Agency quality doesn't justify the cost
- They want visibility and cancel control, not to manage a campaign

### Why this tier is strong

- Highest intent signal in community research
- Faster to convert than founders (clearer ROI frame)
- Higher willingness to pay
- Knows enough SEO to value fit rationale over raw DR

### Budget signal

$500–1,500/mo. Already spending this on agencies or freelancers — willing to switch if trust and quality are higher.

## Secondary ICPs

### Secondary ICP A: SEO agencies needing white-label fulfillment

#### Buyer

- Agency owner
- SEO director
- Head of delivery

#### Why they buy

- Need clean, SaaS-relevant backlink fulfillment they can show clients without embarrassment
- Existing white-label marketplaces use recycled domain lists — clients are noticing
- Need transparent, reviewable fulfillment that doesn't create client trust problems
- Need to expand capacity without hiring more outreach operators

#### The gap they need filled

One Reddit practitioner described their actual manual workflow: "Conduct outreach for a week → update the final list of sites each Friday → let clients approve or reject them → on Mondays we have the confirmed list → we then build links." Mentiohunt automates the discovery-and-outreach half of that loop and gives the agency a live queue to monitor and cancel from, instead of a weekly manual list. The gap is a productized, reliable version of it they can resell.

#### Strengths

- Higher volume potential
- Repeat orders
- B2B pricing, higher willingness to pay per link
- Mentiohunt's monitor-and-cancel workflow already matches what good agencies do manually

#### Risks

- More price-sensitive on margin
- Faster churn if quality slips
- May push toward white-label branding — consider a separate agency SKU rather than mixing with founder ICP messaging

### Secondary ICP B: In-house SEO/content teams at larger B2B or ecommerce brands

#### Buyer

- SEO manager
- Digital marketing manager
- Content marketing manager
- Organic growth manager

#### Why they buy

- Need link acquisition without building outreach ops internally
- Need visibility, cancel control, and reporting

#### Risks

- Longer sales cycle
- More stakeholder buy-in
- Higher expectations on process maturity

### Secondary ICP C: Agency founder building their own authority — VALIDATED, first paying customer

Unlike Secondary ICP A (agency reselling fulfillment to clients), this segment buys Mentiohunt for the agency's **own** site — authority, inbound leads, and positioning, not white-label delivery.

#### Validated example

**Liam Webster — Founder & Managing Director, Identify Digital** (Wakefield, UK; ~10 employees; founded 2012). Digital agency selling web design, development, SEO, and marketing services. First confirmed paying customer in this segment.

#### Buyer

- Agency founder / Managing Director

#### Company profile

- Founder-led digital/marketing/web agency, not a SaaS product company
- Small team (~10 people), established (10+ years), not early-stage
- SEO-aware and growth-oriented already, but still time-constrained
- Sells services (web, e-commerce, integrated marketing), not software

#### Why they buy

- Wants a repeatable way to generate visibility and inbound leads without relying only on referrals
- Wants to position the business as more than a web design shop — build authority around broader digital expertise
- Limited time for manual outreach and link building, despite knowing SEO matters
- Wants marketing effort tied to real business outcomes (leads), not vanity metrics

#### Messaging angle

- "Turn your agency expertise into mentions, backlinks, and inbound opportunities."
- "Get featured in relevant content without manual outreach overload."
- "Build authority for an agency that already knows SEO matters."

#### Qualification summary

- Sophistication: moderate to high
- Budget posture: value-conscious, tool-friendly
- Best use case: authority building + inbound lead generation via mentions and backlinks for the agency's own brand

#### Strengths

- Confirms Mentiohunt's autopilot model works outside strict SaaS, for any founder-led service business with content worth ranking
- Founder-led buying motion matches Tier 1A's fast decision-making and low procurement friction
- Agency-side SEO fluency shortens the "why does fit rationale matter" education step

#### Risks

- Not B2B SaaS — sits outside the core ICP definition in CLAUDE.md; don't let this segment drive early product or messaging decisions
- Distinct from Secondary ICP A's buying motion (own-brand authority vs. client fulfillment) — don't conflate the two in copy or sales scripts
- Landing page currently frames agencies as the competitor/villain ("Zero agency markup"), which reads oddly to an agency-owner buyer — needs a separate landing path or messaging variant before actively pursuing more of this segment

## Who Mentiohunt is not ideal for initially

Avoid making these the default ICP at the start:

- Local businesses with low SEO sophistication
- Broad SMBs that need heavy education before buying
- Buyers looking mainly for cheap links
- Buyers who want fully black-box link buying with no visibility or cancel layer
- Teams expecting social monitoring + community engagement + backlinks in one product

These segments tend to create more support burden, more pricing pressure, and less strategic clarity.
