# Mentiohunt — ICP

## Primary ICP — Tier 1A: Bootstrap / Indie SaaS Founder

**Bootstrapped or pre-Series A SaaS founder who owns SEO personally**

### Buyer

- Founder
- Co-founder

### Company profile

- B2B SaaS, bootstrapped or seed-stage
- 1–10 person team
- Has content or landing pages worth ranking
- Already believes SEO matters
- Cannot justify agency minimums ($1K–5K/mo) and has no time for DIY

### The pricing dead zone this segment falls into

Agency minimums price them out. DIY tactics require 5–15 hrs/week they don't have. Freelancers ghost or deliver junk. No managed, approval-based option exists under $500/mo — that is the gap Mentiohunt fills for this segment.

### Buying triggers

- Organic growth has plateaued
- They tried DIY outreach and burned out
- Hired a freelancer who disappeared or delivered garbage
- Agency quoted $2K–5K/mo minimum — out of budget
- Publishing content but earning no backlinks

### Why this tier is strong

- Very high intent signal — active buying frustration, not passive awareness
- Fast decision-making, no procurement friction
- Founder-to-founder resonance in messaging
- High sensitivity to time savings
- Volume potential as SaaS ecosystem grows

### Budget signal

$200–500/mo. Needs productized tiers. Will pay per link or per month if output is verifiable.

## Primary ICP — Tier 1B: Solo SEO Manager at Sub-$3M ARR B2B SaaS

**Single person owning all of SEO at a lean SaaS company — has the knowledge, lacks the bandwidth**

### Buyer

- SEO Lead
- Head of Growth
- Growth Manager
- Content + SEO

### Company profile

- B2B SaaS, seed to Series A/B
- 5–30 person team
- Has a content program and understands link building strategy
- Cannot justify building outreach ops internally
- Has tried agencies and been disappointed with opacity or quality

### Core pain

They are the single point of failure for a function that requires 20–30 hrs/month of manual work. Tools like Pitchbox and Respona exist but require dedicated operation — they are not self-running. This persona wants to offload execution while retaining approval authority over what gets published under their brand.

### Buying triggers

- Struggling to scale link building without burning out
- Manages prospecting, outreach, negotiation, and verification alone
- Outreach tools require more campaign ownership than they can give
- Agency quality doesn't justify the cost
- They want to approve opportunities, not manage a campaign

### Why this tier is strong

- Highest intent signal in community research
- Faster to convert than founders (clearer ROI frame)
- Higher willingness to pay
- Knows enough SEO to value fit rationale over raw DR

### Budget signal

$500–1,500/mo. Already spending this on agencies or freelancers — willing to switch if trust and quality are higher.

## Secondary ICPs

### Secondary ICP A: SEO agencies needing white-label fulfillment

#### Buyer

- Agency owner
- SEO director
- Head of delivery

#### Why they buy

- Need clean, SaaS-relevant backlink fulfillment they can show clients without embarrassment
- Existing white-label marketplaces use recycled domain lists — clients are noticing
- Need approval-transparent fulfillment that doesn't create client trust problems
- Need to expand capacity without hiring more outreach operators

#### The gap they need filled

One Reddit practitioner described their actual manual workflow: "Conduct outreach for a week → update the final list of sites each Friday → let clients approve or reject them → on Mondays we have the confirmed list → we then build links." That is the Mentiohunt workflow run manually. The gap is a productized, reliable version of it they can resell.

#### Strengths

- Higher volume potential
- Repeat orders
- B2B pricing, higher willingness to pay per link
- Mentiohunt's approval workflow already matches what good agencies do manually

#### Risks

- More price-sensitive on margin
- Faster churn if quality slips
- May push toward white-label branding — consider a separate agency SKU rather than mixing with founder ICP messaging

### Secondary ICP B: In-house SEO/content teams at larger B2B or ecommerce brands

#### Buyer

- SEO manager
- Digital marketing manager
- Content marketing manager
- Organic growth manager

#### Why they buy

- Need link acquisition without building outreach ops internally
- Need visibility, approvals, and reporting

#### Risks

- Longer sales cycle
- More stakeholder buy-in
- Higher expectations on process maturity

## Who Mentiohunt is not ideal for initially

Avoid making these the default ICP at the start:

- Local businesses with low SEO sophistication
- Broad SMBs that need heavy education before buying
- Buyers looking mainly for cheap links
- Buyers who want fully black-box link buying with no approval layer
- Teams expecting social monitoring + community engagement + backlinks in one product

These segments tend to create more support burden, more pricing pressure, and less strategic clarity.
