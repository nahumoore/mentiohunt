# Mentiohunt — ICP

## Primary ICP

**Founder-led B2B SaaS companies with a lean growth/marketing team**

### Buyer

- Founder
- Co-founder
- Head of Growth
- Growth Lead
- SEO Lead at a small team

### User

- Founder directly
- SEO manager
- Content marketer
- Growth marketer

### Company profile

- B2B SaaS
- Seed to Series A/B, or bootstrapped but growth-oriented
- 2–30 person team
- Has content, landing pages, comparison pages, or programmatic pages worth ranking
- Already believes SEO matters
- Cannot justify a full in-house link-building function
- Has tried agencies, freelancers, or manual outreach and disliked the operational overhead

### Buying triggers

- Organic growth has plateaued
- They want more authority for competitive keywords
- They want stronger AI search visibility and branded mentions on relevant sites
- They are publishing content but not earning enough backlinks
- They are disappointed with agency quality or lack of transparency
- They know backlinks matter but do not want to operate outreach internally

### Why this ICP is strongest

- Clear pain
- Fast decision-making
- Lower procurement friction
- High sensitivity to time savings
- Strong desire for control without operational involvement
- Easier founder-to-founder resonance in messaging

## Secondary ICPs

### Secondary ICP A: SEO agencies / resellers

#### Buyer

- Agency owner
- SEO director
- Head of delivery

#### Why they buy

- Need reliable backlink fulfillment capacity
- Need to avoid hiring/training more outreach operators
- Need margin, repeatability, and client-friendly reporting

#### Strengths

- Higher volume potential
- Repeat orders
- Easy upsell into fulfillment partnerships

#### Risks

- More price-sensitive on margin
- Faster churn if quality slips
- May push toward white-label and custom process demands

### Secondary ICP B: In-house SEO/content teams at larger B2B or ecommerce brands

#### Buyer

- SEO manager
- Digital marketing manager
- Content marketing manager
- Organic growth manager

#### Why they buy

- Need link acquisition without building outreach ops internally
- Need visibility, approvals, and reporting

#### Risks

- Longer sales cycle
- More stakeholder buy-in
- Higher expectations on process maturity

---

## Who Mentiohunt is not ideal for initially

Avoid making these the default ICP at the start:

- Local businesses with low SEO sophistication
- Broad SMBs that need heavy education before buying
- Buyers looking mainly for cheap links
- Buyers who want fully black-box link buying with no approval layer
- Teams expecting social monitoring + community engagement + backlinks in one product

These segments tend to create more support burden, more pricing pressure, and less strategic clarity.
