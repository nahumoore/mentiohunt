## Why this cluster

- KD 0–7 across the board, ~15–38 referring domains needed to rank (vs. 100–600+ on head SEO terms like "link building software").
- Live SERP has zero Ahrefs/Semrush/Moz presence — competing pages are Mailtrap, Hunter.io, Instantly, QuickMail, Overloop, Backlinko (a templates sub-page, not their pillar). Beatable tier.
- Gap in SERP: every competitor does one big "13 templates" dump page. Nobody has dedicated per-scenario pages — that's the wedge.
- Direct product tie-in: Mentiohunt generates these drafts for real prospects, not static copy-paste templates.
- Existing proof of life: `/blog/link-building-outreach-email` already pulls 195 impressions at pos 43.9 (0 clicks) — real unanswered demand, nothing dedicated ranking for it yet.
