Reddinbox is another product built by the same founder as Mentiohunt — an audience intelligence tool with no dedicated SEO or outreach hire. Its backlinks are managed through Mentiohunt itself, which means this isn't a sanitized third-party testimonial. It's the real dashboard numbers, good and unglamorous parts included.

Over 6 months, Reddinbox earned 159 backlinks running Mentiohunt's automated discovery and outreach. Most of those links targeted one specific keyword: "best gummy search alternative." Not long after, Reddinbox started showing up in Google's AI Overview for that exact query.

![Ahrefs backlink profile showing Reddinbox's 159 earned backlinks and referring domains](/resources/case-studies/reddinbox/ahrefs-proof.webp)

## Where Reddinbox started

Reddinbox needed backlinks the way most young SaaS products do — no existing authority, no PR budget, and no time for a founder to manually prospect sites and write cold emails one at a time.

Instead of spreading outreach thin across many loosely related keywords, the campaign concentrated on one: "best gummy search alternative." Every discovery method ran against that target, and outreach emails were written around content that specifically fit that query.

## The approach: five discovery methods, one keyword focus

Mentiohunt rotates through five automated discovery methods, one per day per product, so the queue keeps filling without manual research:

| Method                       | What it does                                                                                                          |
| ---------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Competitor Backlink          | Pulls [competitors' backlink profiles](/free-tools/competitor-backlink-gap) and scores the linking pages as prospects |
| Unlinked Mention             | Finds pages that already mention the product but don't link to it yet                                                 |
| Listicle & Roundup Inclusion | Finds "best X" / "top N" roundup articles missing the product                                                         |
| Resource Page Inclusion      | Finds [curated resource pages](/blog/resource-page-link-building) that match the product's own page keywords          |
| Broken Link Building         | Finds [dead links on relevant sites](/free-tools/broken-link-finder) and suggests a live page as the replacement      |

There's also a manual option for submitting a specific URL directly, used for one-off targets a founder spots themselves.

Every qualified opportunity got a [ready-to-send outreach email](/free-tools/backlink-outreach-email-generator), sent automatically: one initial email plus two follow-ups. The moment a prospect replied, automation stopped — Reddinbox's founder took the conversation from there personally, the same way Mentiohunt works for any product.

## The results

A 5% reply rate isn't unusual for [cold outreach](/blog/link-building-outreach-email) — most of the work here was volume and targeting, not a hidden trick. What mattered more than the count was the concentration: nearly all 159 backlinks pointed at content built around "best gummy search alternative," instead of being scattered across a dozen loosely related pages.

## Then the AI Overview citation

Within that 6-month window, Reddinbox started appearing in Google's AI Overview for "best gummy search alternative."

![Google AI Overview citing Reddinbox for the search query "best gummy search alternative"](/resources/case-studies/reddinbox/ai-overview-proof.webp)

Worth being precise about what this does and doesn't prove: backlinks aren't the dominant factor search engines use to decide AI Overview citations — brand mentions and topical concentration correlate more strongly, based on aggregate research covered in [our breakdown of backlinks and AI search citations](/blog/backlinks-ai-search-citations). What Reddinbox's campaign did was build both at once, on purpose, around a single query, rather than optimizing for total backlink count.

## What this doesn't prove

This is one company and one keyword. It's not a guarantee that 159 concentrated backlinks will earn any product an AI Overview citation, and Mentiohunt doesn't promise citations, rankings, or replies for any campaign. What it shows is that a small, founder-run product can build enough topical signal around one target without hiring an agency or a full-time SEO person — and that concentrating outreach around a single keyword, rather than spreading it thin, is what made the difference here.

Reddinbox's queue ran the same way any Mentiohunt product does: point it at your article URLs or competitors, let the daily [discovery methods](/blog/how-to-find-backlink-opportunities) fill the queue, and review what comes back.
