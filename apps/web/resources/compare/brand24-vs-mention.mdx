## What Each Tool Does

**[Brand24](https://brand24.com)** is an AI-powered social listening platform that monitors 25 million+ online sources — social media, news, blogs, forums, podcasts, reviews, Reddit, TikTok, LinkedIn, and more — in real time. When a keyword you're tracking appears anywhere, it lands in your dashboard with sentiment classification, reach estimates, and engagement data. The AI layer goes further than most: AI Insights surfaces actionable suggestions from your mention data, AI Brand Assistant helps you generate reports faster, and Storm Alerts fire when mention volume spikes unexpectedly.

**[Mention](https://mention.com)** is a social listening and media monitoring tool that claims coverage across 1 billion+ sources including web, social, news, and 75+ review sites. It's been a fixture in PR and marketing teams for years, with real-time alerts, boolean search, sentiment analysis, and a basic social media management inbox built in. The source count is higher on paper, but the counting methodology is different — the comparison isn't as simple as bigger-number-wins.

The meaningful difference: Brand24 is analytics-forward with a fast-evolving AI layer. Mention is monitoring-forward with a longer history in traditional PR use cases.

## Who Each Tool Is Actually Built For

**Brand24** fits best for:

- Founders and small marketing teams tracking brand reputation without a PR department
- Startups that need to start monitoring in hours, not days, and want tiered pricing they can grow into
- Agencies running multiple client projects with white-label reports on Pro and above
- PR teams that need crisis detection (Storm Alerts) paired with AI-powered analytics

**Mention** fits best for:

- PR and communications teams where traditional media coverage matters alongside social
- Marketing managers who want a longer evaluation window before committing
- Teams already using Meltwater who are looking for something less expensive and less contractually locked in

Both tools will tell you something was mentioned. Neither helps you figure out what to do about it — no fit rationale, no outreach prep, no suggested replies for the communities where the mentions happen.

## Core Feature Breakdown

### Pricing and Plans

This is where the tools diverge most clearly.

Brand24 publishes four tiers with specific limits at each level: Individual ($199/month annual), Team ($299), Pro ($399), Business ($599). The upgrade logic is straightforward — more keywords, more mentions per month, real-time updates, and AI features unlock as you go up. No contracts, cancel anytime, 30-day money-back guarantee on paid plans.

Mention's pricing is harder to nail down. The Company Plan at $599/month (billed yearly) is the primary offering clearly documented in public sources. Some third-party sources list Solo ($29/month) and Starter ($99/month) plans, but these appear to be outdated or inconsistently available. The official pricing page requires interaction to see full details. Users report frustration with upsells and feature gates — traditional media sources require additional payment on top of the base plan.

If you're a small team trying to compare costs before booking a demo, Brand24 is easier to evaluate.

### AI Features

Brand24 has invested heavily here. The current AI layer includes:
- **AI Sentiment Analysis** — positive/negative/neutral across 108 languages
- **AI Brand Assistant** — generates report summaries, answers questions about your data
- **AI Insights** — surfaces actionable suggestions from mention patterns (Pro and up)
- **AI Topics** — clusters mentions by topic automatically (Pro and up)
- **AI Events Detection** — explains spikes in coverage (Pro and up)
- **AI Visibility** — tracks your brand exposure in ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews (paid add-on)

Mention's AI layer is thinner. Sentiment analysis has been there for a while. AI emotion detection — which goes beyond positive/negative into emotional tones — was added in 2024 but is described as a newer feature with limited historical data. The gap between the two tools on AI depth is significant.

### Source Coverage

Mention claims 1 billion+ sources. Brand24 claims 25 million+. This sounds like a clear Mention win, but the numbers use different counting methodologies and aren't directly comparable. What matters more is whether each tool covers the specific sources you care about.

Both cover the major platforms: X/Twitter, Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, TikTok, YouTube, Reddit, Pinterest, news sites, blogs, and forums. Brand24 adds Twitch, Spotify, and podcast monitoring. Mention adds 75+ review sites including G2. For most founders and marketing teams, both tools cover enough ground.

### Trial Terms

Brand24: 14 days, no credit card required, 30-day money-back guarantee on paid plans. You can start tracking the same day and cancel with no friction if it doesn't fit.

Mention: 30 days, which is a longer window. However, users report that the free version is "too limited, only focused on web listening functions," which means the trial may not give you a full picture of the paid product.

### Reporting

Brand24 generates automated reports in PDF, Excel, and PowerPoint. White-label reports are available on Pro and above. Users report the reports save 10–15 hours of manual work per month.

Mention has reporting but users consistently note it "still lacks world-class reporting" and that report customization feels limited. There's no Excel or PowerPoint equivalent on entry-level plans. White-label is available on the Company Plan.

### Social Media Management

Mention has basic social publishing built in — you can respond to mentions and schedule posts from the same dashboard. Users rate it as functional but "okay, not best in class." It's useful if you want to consolidate tools, but it's not a reason to choose Mention over a dedicated scheduling tool.

Brand24 doesn't offer social publishing. It's a listening and analytics tool only.

## Where Both Tools Fall Short

**Monitoring without action.** Both tools are excellent at surfacing what's being said about your brand. Neither helps you decide what to do about it. You get alerts; you don't get a queue of opportunities with fit rationale and a suggested next step.

**Noise in results.** Brand24 users report irrelevant mentions when keywords are common. Mention users report similar issues — "sometimes even too much with a lot of noise." Both tools require manual filtering to remove low-relevance results.

**Sentiment accuracy.** Brand24 claims its AI "definitely gets sarcasm" but user reviews contradict this for sarcastic, mixed-emotion, and non-English content. Mention's sentiment is described as functional but not exceptional. Neither should be trusted for nuanced emotional analysis without manual review.

**Community engagement.** Both tools alert you when someone mentions your brand in a forum or on Reddit. Neither suggests what to reply, helps you understand if the post is worth engaging with, or prepares a response while the thread is still active.

**API costs extra.** Brand24's API is a $99 add-on for non-Enterprise plans. Mention's API is also gated — only 250-character snippets are returned, historical data can't be accessed via API, and access costs extra. Neither is a good fit for teams wanting to pipe mention data into custom workflows at the entry price.

## The Bottom Line

**Brand24** is the stronger pick for most founders and small marketing teams. The tiered pricing is transparent, the AI features are genuinely useful, and the trial terms (no credit card, 30-day money-back) mean you can evaluate it without risk. The Individual plan at $199/month is a real entry point, not a teaser.

**Mention** makes sense if you specifically need a longer evaluation window, your primary workflow is PR and media monitoring rather than social analytics, or you need basic social publishing in the same tool. But the pricing confusion is a real obstacle — you may spend more time trying to figure out what plan you're actually on than you do evaluating whether the tool fits.

If you're a founder doing your own distribution and looking for something that goes beyond monitoring into actual opportunity discovery, neither tool is built for that. The complexity is real, the price is real for what you get, and the alerts still leave you with an empty queue and no clear next step.
